Two of these matter more than the rest.

**`commercial-before-after-split.png` is the hero**, at the agency's direction. The
composition puts the failed roof behind the headline and the restored roof to the right of
it, so the page argues its own positioning before a word is read.

building, the same aerial angle, weathered and then coated. That is the one thing a
before/after slider needs and the one thing stock can never supply, so it now drives the
first slider on the page.

## A note on the Drive library

It is AI-generated rather than photographed, and it is predominantly residential: of roughly
100 files, about ten are commercial or flat-roofing, and those ten are now all in use. The
residential shingle, siding and window images are not usable on a commercial-only site.

`Roofing logo.png` in the folder is a generic "YOUR ROOFING COMPANY" template mark, not this
client's logo. The site uses a type-set wordmark instead.

Everything here is still a placeholder for real photography of James's own completed work.
