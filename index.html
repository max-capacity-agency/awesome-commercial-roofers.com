Before any price exists

Repair, restore, or replace: how we decide

Almost every commercial roof we survey falls into one of three buckets. The moisture readings decide which one, not the sales conversation.

  1. Deck dry, damage isolated

    Targeted repair

    A failed seam, a split at a penetration, flashing pulled off a parapet. We fix the defect and leave the rest of the roof alone.

  2. Deck dry, surface worn

    Full restoration

    The membrane or the metal has weathered out but the assembly underneath is sound and the wet area is under 🚩⁠25% of the field. This is the coating system, installed over your existing roof.

  3. Wet insulation or failed deck

    Tear-off, and we will say so

    Once saturated insulation runs past 🚩⁠25% of the field, or the deck itself has gone, a coating seals the water in instead of fixing the problem. We tell you that, and we do not quote you a restoration.

You get the survey readings and marked-up photographs from your own roof before any price exists. If your roof is a tear-off, you will hear it from us first.

The systems

What we install on New Mexico roofs

Four systems, and the survey decides which one your roof gets. Each has a real tradeoff, and here they are.

Acrylic coating system

🚩⁠10 to 15 year service life, renewable

A water-based acrylic built up over a prepared roof in 🚩⁠two coats at 🚩⁠30 mils dry. It reflects most of the sun that lands on it, which matters at this elevation where the UV load is high most of the year.

The tradeoff: acrylic needs the water to run off. On a roof that holds standing water after a storm it stays soft, so we specify silicone there instead. It is the least expensive of the four per square foot.

Silicone coating system

🚩⁠15 to 20 year service life, renewable

A moisture-cure silicone, specified where a roof ponds or where the drainage cannot be corrected. It holds up under standing water in a way acrylic does not, and it cures in humidity rather than fighting it.

The tradeoff: silicone holds dirt, so the bright white reflectivity drops off over the first few years. Recoating later needs a silicone-compatible primer, and it costs more per square foot than acrylic.

Spray polyurethane foam

🚩⁠20 year service life once coated

Foam is what we use when the problem is the slope rather than the surface. It is sprayed to build fall toward the drains, it seals around every penetration as one piece, and it adds insulation value across the whole deck.

The tradeoff: foam has to be coated to survive UV, and it takes damage from hail and from anything dropped on it. It needs a maintenance program rather than being left alone for a decade.

Metal roof restoration

🚩⁠15 to 20 year service life

Rust treated or removed, fasteners replaced and sealed, then every seam detailed with reinforcing fabric before the panel field gets coated as one continuous surface. The roof stops being a thousand separate leak points.

The tradeoff: if the panels have rusted through or the purlins have gone, coating over the top hides a structural problem instead of solving it. We check that first, and sometimes the honest answer is new metal.

How it works

From survey to warranty

  1. /01

    We walk the roof, cut test samples where we need to, and run a moisture scan across the field. You get the readings and the marked-up photographs, not a verbal summary at the truck.

  2. /02

    Every line item, the system specified, the deck repair rate and the price. That is the price you pay unless you ask us to change the scope.

  3. /03

    Access, staging, parking and working hours are agreed before anyone shows up. 🚩⁠We work evenings and weekends where a tenant needs it.

  4. /04

    The slow part, and the part that decides whether the roof lasts. Cleaning, rust treatment or primer, then every seam, drain, curb and penetration detailed by hand before a field coat goes anywhere.

  5. /05

    Field coats go on at the specified thickness and we check it as we go. You get the warranty paperwork and the final photographs on the day we leave.
